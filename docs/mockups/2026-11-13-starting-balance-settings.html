Starting balance in Settings: bank and credit card

The starting point (the balance on the day the app starts counting) can be changed in the account's Settings. Changing it moves every balance after that day by the difference, so Save asks first. Adjust on the account page stays for later counts: it adds a correction row and leaves the start as it was.

1 · Bank → Settings

Balance becomes a row that opens the keypad (today it is read only). As of works as today.
