Card statement: purchases covered by a payment

A payment made with the card page’s Pay covers the statement’s purchases oldest first, and every covered purchase shows as paid — no ticking needed. Purchases paid one by one (tick → Pay) keep their dark ✓; covered ones get a soft green ✓. Nothing about money changes, only what the list shows. Amounts and names are made up.
