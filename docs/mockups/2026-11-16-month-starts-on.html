"Month starts on": a month that begins on payday

Example: salary on the 25th. Every page that shows a month uses the same setting. A period is named after the month most of it falls in: 25 Oct – 24 Nov is "November". Invented figures. Green "NEW" = what is added; the rest of each page stays as it is.

3 · What changes, and what does not

Cashflow, Budget, Essential · Lifestyle · Debts & savings
Count the chosen period instead of the calendar month
Recurring bills
A bill keeps its due day; it counts in the period its date falls in
Financial health, reports, "this month" on other pages
Use the same periods, so every page agrees
Card statements, loans, goals' due dates
Unchanged — they follow the bank's or the plan's own dates
Transactions
Never moved or changed; changing the setting only regroups them
