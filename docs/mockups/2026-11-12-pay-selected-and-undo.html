Pay selected purchases, and Undo after paying

Two screens. (1) On a credit card's statement, ticking purchases opens a "Pay N purchases now" form at the very bottom of the list, out of sight. (2) On a bill, paying or linking a purchase shows a green "✓ Paid … Undo" box, the only green Undo in the app. Amounts and names here are made up.
