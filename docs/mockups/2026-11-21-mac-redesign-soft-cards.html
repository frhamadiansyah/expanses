Tab bar, left to right: Overview · Cashflow · Budget · Wallet (accounts, cards, points) · Net worth · Plans (goals, events, debt-free plan) · Review (with its count) · Settings. Each lights up in its own colour when open. What moves: Bills goes to the Overview card and Budget; Points and the account list go inside Wallet; Goals, Events and Debt-free plan go under Plans. Trade-off: today's sidebar reaches every page in one click; the tab bar needs two clicks for Bills, Points and single accounts.
