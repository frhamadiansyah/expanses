What each one means

CircleMeansGets it whenA tap
1Not paid yetBilled, nothing paid toward itPicks it to pay
2Picked to pay (not paid yet)Tapped just now; the black “Pay 1 purchase” bar appearsUnpicks it
3Paid one by one (black)Paid through “Pay N purchases” for just theseShows the pay date
4Covered by a payment (light green)A card payment of the whole bill (or enough of it) was recorded; cicis counts it from the oldest purchaseSays which payment covered it
5Paid off before cicis (solid green)Marked “Paid off” in the ⋯ menu — paid outside the appOffers “Not paid off”
6Settled (a refund) / Paid (an instalment)Same solid green as 5, with its own word in the ⋯ menuOffers “Not settled” / “Not paid”

So a paid purchase can wear three different ✓ today: black (3), light green (4) and solid green (5, 6). All three mean “nothing left to pay on this one”; they differ only in how it was paid.
