Wallet: Balance after bills

Wallet’s Balance already takes off what the credit cards owe (whole balance, billed or not) and what goals have set aside. Proposed: it also takes off the bills still unpaid this month. Nothing to set up. Each bank and card keeps its real balance, exactly as the bank shows it — only the one Balance figure at the top changes. Names and amounts are made up.
