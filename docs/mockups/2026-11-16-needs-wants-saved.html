Needs, wants and saved: where to see them

Every category is already marked Essential (a need) or Lifestyle (a want); unmarked counts as a need. Saved = income minus spending, so money moved to savings, goals or investments counts as saved. Invented month: income Rp 25.000.000 · needs Rp 13.000.000 · wants Rp 7.750.000 · saved Rp 4.250.000.
