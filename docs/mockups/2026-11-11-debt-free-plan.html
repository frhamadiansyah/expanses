Our edge over the debt-only appsPayments from bank messages and emails tick instalments off by themselves. Cards come with real statements. Household debts can be shared. Data stays on the phone; no Google account.
Where the numbers come fromEach loan's own schedule (terms, rate, months left), card statements, recurring bills, and Lend & borrow. A debt with no months set has no end month, so the plan says "debt-free date unknown" until one is added.
Left out on purposeRisk levels (late + interest already say it), daily challenges, and any Google Drive or email set-up.
