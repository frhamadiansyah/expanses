Mark pasted card purchases as paid off

Card purchases pasted from Notion or Google Sheets that were already paid, so the card does not owe them. Today Paid off is one row at a time on the card's statement. Two ways to do many at once, shown on the Mac. New parts outlined in orange. Invented card and figures.

A · On the statement: Select, then Paid off SUGGESTED

After the paste is recorded, open the card. Select ticks rows; the bar at the bottom gets Paid off beside Pay.
