Transfers: one rule

Only for transfers (money moving between accounts). Spending and income are not affected. Nothing is deleted or moved: balances, net worth, card statements and account pages stay exactly as today. Names and figures invented.

Does the transfer involve someone else's money?
(the husband's account, or a joint account)
↓ No: only my own accounts↓ Yes
NO → my own move

Not in Cashflow

  • Shows on the card statement and the account page, as today.
  • Cashflow → Filter → Transfers shows them when needed.
Examples:
💳
Card payment
my bank 0917 → my card 4821
⇄
Top-up
my bank 0917 → my GoPay
💱
Currency exchange
my Cash IDR → my Cash TRY
YES → household money

In Family's Cashflow only

  • Both phones see it in Family.
  • Not in Personal or Business.
Examples:
⇄
To joint account
my bank 0917 → Joint 2210
⇄
To husband
my card 4821 → his GoPay
💳
Joint card payment
Joint 2210 → joint card 7710

Two extras (already agreed)

+
A fee I type in Fees & charges is spending: it always shows in Cashflow (and on the trip, if the transfer moved there).
+
Moved into a trip: the transfer shows on the trip page as grey "not counted". A rate above market (no fee typed) counts nowhere.

What will feel different

1
Card payments and top-ups disappear from Cashflow. To check one: open the card or the bank account, or turn on the Transfers filter.
2
A captured card-payment SMS, once confirmed, is not in Cashflow. The confirmation says "Saved on card 4821's statement".
3
To move my own exchange (Cash IDR → Cash TRY) into a trip: turn on the Transfers filter first, then Select → Add to trip.
4
Unchanged: balances, net worth, card statements, account pages, bills, Debt-free plan, monthly totals. Only what Cashflow lists changes.
