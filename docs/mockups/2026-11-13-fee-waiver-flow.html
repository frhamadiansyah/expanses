Fee waiver: the whole flow

Invented figures. Orange edge = new. Settings keeps 3 rows (Annual fee, Charged on, Fee waiver). Tapping Fee waiver opens this page: Program on top (same choices as today), 2026 fee under it, filled in from the card's own transactions. No Result field: the app learns from the card whether its bank skips the fee or charges then reverses it, and expects the same next year. Progress uses bar + words (option C); a reversal shows only once it is on the card (option A).
