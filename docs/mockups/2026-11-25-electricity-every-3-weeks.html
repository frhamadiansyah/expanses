Electricity token as a bill every 3 weeks

Rp 1.000.000 every 3 weeks: 3 Nov, 24 Nov, 15 Dec, 5 Jan, 26 Jan … Some months have two top-ups, some one. Bills today repeat monthly, yearly or every few months, never by weeks. This joins A (each top-up shows in Cashflow on the day it was bought) with a schedule the Budget can follow. New parts outlined in orange. Invented figures.
