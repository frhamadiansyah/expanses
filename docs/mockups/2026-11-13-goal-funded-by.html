Goal page: Funded by, and Set aside

Two fixes. Funded by: a multicurrency account is one row (its total in the goal's currency), with its currencies under it, not one row per currency. Set aside: each account says plainly how much is free to use, with "Use all", and the sheet says how much the goal still needs. Names and figures invented. Goal: "Hajj 2030", target Rp 120.000.000.

1 · Funded by

Today every currency of a multicurrency account is its own row, each with its own conversion, so one account looks like three. After: one row per account; tap ▾ to see its currencies.
