Why not its own tabThe bar is full: Transactions, Net worth, ＋, Wallet, Account. The Cashflow row and the reminders already bring debts forward when something is due, which is what a tab would be for.
Quiet when nothing is dueThe Cashflow row hides when nothing is late or due within 3 days. Someone with no debts never sees any of it.
Liabilities untouchedAssets and Liabilities stay twins. The plan never sits inside Liabilities; at most a corner icon (2), the same way Income sits behind the Assets corner.
