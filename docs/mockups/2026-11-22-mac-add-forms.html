A fits long forms (Add transaction, Add holding, Edit budgets, Goal) and screens that are pages anyway (Goal detail, Card statement, Categories). B feels lighter for short adds but long forms would scroll inside the sheet.

Either way, for every other phone screen on the Mac

About 40 screens still show the phone layout on the Mac, for example Goal detail, Edit budgets, Assets, Card statement, Card settings, Event plan, Loan calculator, Categories, Workspace, Capture settings, Tax report and Import. Step 1 gives all of them the Mac frame at once: the Mac top bar with ‹ back and the page's buttons on the right, the thin notice lines, and boxes that use the width. Then the add and edit forms get A or B, one group at a time.
