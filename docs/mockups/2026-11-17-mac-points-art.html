Mac redesign · step 9: Points, with card art

Invented programmes. KrisFlyer and Marriott Bonvoy (Silver Elite) are the app's own drawn membership cards, the same ones the iPhone shows. A bank's own points (BCA Rewards, CIMB, Maybank, Jenius) have no membership card, so here they wear that bank's card art; on iPhone today they are a plain face.
