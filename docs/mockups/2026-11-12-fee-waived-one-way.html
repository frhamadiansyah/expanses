Annual fee: one way to say it came back

One sheet, Fee waived. Option A opens it from two places: the “This year’s fee” row in Settings and the fee challenge on the card. Option B opens it from Settings only. It fits a fee the app recorded and a fee charged before the app was used. “Waived” and “Fee refunded” as two separate things go away. Names and amounts are made up.
