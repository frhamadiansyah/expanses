Mac Cashflow · paste option D: a paste window

Nothing lands in the month list while it is being fixed. ⌘V opens a window with the pasted rows; fix them there (several cells at once), then Add. They go straight to their own months. Invented rows.

D · ⌘V opens "Paste 4 transactions"

No amber, no orange boxes: like Notion or Sheets, a missing cell is simply blank. A ✓ marks a complete row, a light ○ one with a blank. The two blank Category cells are selected together (blue) and get one choice. "Add 2 ready" adds what is complete and keeps the rest open; "Add all 4" when everything is filled. Cancel throws the paste away.
