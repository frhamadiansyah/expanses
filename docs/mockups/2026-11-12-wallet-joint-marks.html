Partner’s joint cards in Wallet: where the mark can go

Drawn on the app’s real card layout. Taken: top-left the bank, top-right the amount, middle-left the chip and contactless, bottom-left the last 4 digits and the type, bottom-right Visa / Mastercard. Free: the middle-right beside the chip, and the line a real card uses for the holder’s name. No outline round the card in any option. Andi’s phone; the 2nd and 4th cards are Rina’s joint ones. Names and amounts are made up.

The stack (pile) view shows only each card’s top strip (bank and amount) until it is opened, so a mark that is not in the top strip is hidden there — see the last section.
