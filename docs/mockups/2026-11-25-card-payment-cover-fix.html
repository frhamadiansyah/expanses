A card payment taken by purchases added later

A payment made without ticking purchases covers the statement oldest first. Purchases added later with an earlier date take it, and a covered row has no Paid off, so the card shows more owed than the bank. Two fixes; they work together. New parts outlined in orange. Invented card, shops and figures.

The 24 Nov payment (1.000.000) was for the three on 23 Nov. Kios Buah and Apotek were added later, dated 22 Nov, and were already paid elsewhere, but the payment covers oldest first, so it went to them. No Paid off on a covered row.
