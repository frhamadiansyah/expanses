Card in credit: the back says Credit, and the credit adds to what is available

Invented example: BCA Visa, limit Rp 100.000.000. Paid Rp 50.000.000 more than owed (or a big refund / cashback), so the bank holds Rp 50.000.000 credit. TODAY = real screenshots. AFTER = drawn; green box = what changes.

Card in credit

"Owed now · Rp 0" plus a "Credit Rp 50.000.000" line becomes one figure: Credit · Rp 50.000.000. Available counts the credit too: the bank lets the card spend the limit plus the credit, so Rp 150.000.000 available of Rp 100.000.000. The limit itself stays Rp 100.000.000.
