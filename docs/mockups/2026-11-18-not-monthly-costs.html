iPhone · not-monthly costs: bills every few months, and "Year only" on any purchase

Invented: a car service bill every 6 months (~Rp 1.800.000), and new tyres Rp 4.350.000 that are not a bill. Build 149's rule widens from "a yearly bill" to "a bill that comes less than once a month, or a purchase marked Year only". In a month: listed, greyed, not in the ring or total. In a year and every report: counted.

1 · A bill every 6 months

Nothing new to fill in: a bill set to repeat every 2 months or more (or yearly) is a not-monthly cost on its own. The form says so under Repeats.

The receipt gets one row: Counts in · Month / Year only. Month is the default. Year only for things like tyres, a laptop or school fees.
