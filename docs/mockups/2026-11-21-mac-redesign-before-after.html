cicis for Mac · before and after, page by page

Left: today’s Mac layout with the sample data, at 1280×820. Right: the soft-cards-with-a-sidebar proposal (2026-11-21-mac-redesign-sidebar.html) at the same size, with its own invented figures, so the numbers differ. Orange dots mark what changed; the list under each pair says what.

Overview

  1. 1The page opens on a large title with a grey line under it; the toolbar keeps only the page name.
  2. 2Money on hand and How long it lasts give way to four stat tiles: spent, received, left to spend, net worth.
  3. 3Worth a look becomes Bills this month: one coloured dot and bar per status.
  4. 4The account list leaves the sidebar (Accounts › opens it). Each sidebar page has its own icon and lights up in its own colour.
  5. 5New: Quick actions, four pastel tiles for the common jobs. (after only)

Cashflow

  1. 1Large title over the page.
  2. 2The donut, the daily bars and the This month box become four stat tiles; To review carries a badge.
  3. 3Transactions sit in one soft card: one black Record pill in its header, tick circles in the first column.
  4. 4Filter and the search field become one round search button.

Budget

  1. 1Large title over the page.
  2. 2The gauge and the split bar become three stat tiles: Essential, Lifestyle, Debts & savings, each with what is left.
  3. 3The category spreadsheet becomes one card per group, each row with a progress bar and the amount left.
  4. 4Over budget reads as “Rp 110.000 over” in red with a full red bar.

Bills

  1. 1Large title over the page.
  2. 2The calendar and summary boxes become four stat tiles by status: Paid, Due this week, Later this month, Late.
  3. 3One table for the month: a coloured status dot on the left and a coloured tag on the right (Paid green, Due soon blue, Late red).
  4. 4Yearly set-aside, card fees and instalments are not drawn in the mockup yet. (before only)

Review

  1. 1Large title over the page.
  2. 2The count strip becomes three stat tiles: waiting, ready to record, missing something.
  3. 3Record and Discard on every row give way to a tick circle per row (filled = ready, dashed = missing something) and one black Record 2 pill.
  4. 4The source chip (Email, Import, Notification) becomes a grey line under the name.
  5. 5New: a Message card beside the list shows the captured text and what was read from it. (after only)

Wallet

  1. 1Large title over the page.
  2. 2Banks and cash become four account tiles with their balance; the Balance and Where the money sits boxes go.
  3. 3Credit cards show as gradient faces with the amount owed and the pay-by date under each.
  4. 4Points moves from a toolbar button to a Points card on the page.
  5. 5Card payments: what each card needs and when, in one list.

Net worth

  1. 1Large title over the page.
  2. 2Total, the year line and assets vs debts share one card; Financial health is not on this page.
  3. 3The treemaps become grouped rows with a total per group.
  4. 4New: debts against assets as one bar. (after only)

Credit card page

  1. 1Large title with the card name; digits and statement days on the grey line under it.
  2. 2The outstanding amount moves onto a big card face, with credit left.
  3. 3The statement box reads Spent, Paid, Left to pay, with a Pay by tag.
  4. 4Each row shows its payment: one green ✓ with “Paid 3 Nov” or “Paid with the 15 Nov payment”; unpaid rows keep an empty grey ring.
  5. 5Toolbar: Pay credit card and New expense instead of Spend, Statement and Pay.
