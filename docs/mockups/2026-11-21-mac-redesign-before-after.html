cicis for Mac · before, v1 and v2, page by page

Left: today’s Mac layout with the sample data, at 1280×820. Middle: v1, the soft-cards-with-a-sidebar proposal (2026-11-21-mac-redesign-sidebar.html). Right: v2 (2026-11-21-mac-redesign-v2.html), keeping everything from today and from v1, with every part (charts, lists, tables, buttons, calendar) redrawn in v1’s look; its pages are taller and shown at full height. Each mockup uses its own invented figures, so the numbers differ. Orange dots mark what changed from today to v1; “v2 keeps” lists what v2 brings back from today and what it keeps from v1.

Overview

Before → v1

  1. 1The page opens on a large title with a grey line under it; the toolbar keeps only the page name.
  2. 2Money on hand and How long it lasts give way to four stat tiles: spent, received, left to spend, net worth.
  3. 3Worth a look becomes Bills this month: one coloured dot and bar per status.
  4. 4The account list leaves the sidebar (Accounts › opens it). Each sidebar page has its own icon and lights up in its own colour.
  5. 5New: Quick actions, four pastel tiles for the common jobs. (after only)

v2 keeps

  • From todayMoney on hand with its monthly bar chart, legend and balance-ahead line; the date range.
  • From todayHow long it lasts gauge with spent a month and target.
  • From todayAccounts with balances and bars, Worth a look, Next 14 days.
  • From todayThe account list, Tools and the backup line in the sidebar.
  • From v1Four stat tiles, Bills this month dots and bars, Recent with Recorded / To review tags, Quick actions.
