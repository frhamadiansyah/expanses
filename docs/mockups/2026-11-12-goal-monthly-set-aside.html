Goal: set the Monthly amount aside by itself

Today a goal’s Monthly amount is only a plan figure: it adds to “Set up a month” but nothing happens to any account. Proposed: one switch in the Monthly sheet sets that amount aside each month, in an account picked once, on a chosen day. Money stays in the account; it moves from Free to spend to Set aside, exactly as a hand-made set-aside does today. Names and amounts are made up.
