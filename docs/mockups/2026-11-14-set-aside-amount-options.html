Set aside: the amount step, simpler

Five ways to type the amount after picking an account. All of them: the number pad is up straight away, and Save is blocked (grey) while the amount is more than is free. Invented figures: Mandiri Tabungan, Rp 6.500.000 free, goal "Hajj 2030".

Currency: "≈ Rp …" only shows when the account's money differs from the goal's. A USD goal from a USD account shows dollars only.

1 · Just the number Recommended

One big number and one line under it: what is free, and "All" to fill it. Nothing else.

Typing

Free line grey, "All" tappable.
