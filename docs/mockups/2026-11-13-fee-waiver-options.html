Fee waiver: the small things

Invented figures. Done as asked: "Not charged yet" → Pending; "Spent so far" → Progress. Below: what replaces Result, ✓ / missed, and the waiting lines.

1 · "Result" goes away: the app learns it from the card

Right: a Spending challenge is always charged, then reversed, so it never needs Result. For a yearly amount or Free for life, the first year shows what this card does: no charge on 15 Oct means the bank skips it; a charge then a reversal means it reverses. The next year expects the same. No field to set.
