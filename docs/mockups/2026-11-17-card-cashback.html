Cashback on a credit card: Cashback & Rewards, not income

Invented example: Rp 75.000 promo cashback onto BCA Visa; a Rp 320.000 supermarket purchase on the same card. TODAY = screenshots of the real app as it is now. AFTER = the same screens with the change; green box = what changes. The rule: cashback into a credit card lowers total spending; it never adds to income. Into a bank, cash or e-wallet it stays income, as today.
