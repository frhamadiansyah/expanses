Cashback on a credit card: Cashback & Rewards, not income

Invented example: a promo gives Rp 75.000 cashback on BCA Visa on 20 Nov. November purchases add up to Rp 8.450.000. Red = taps. Green NEW = what is added. The rule: cashback into a credit card lowers the month's total spending; it never adds to income. Cashback into a bank, cash or e-wallet stays income, as today.

Adding it

Typed the same way as a refund (Expense, minus first, Into the card). Once the money goes into a credit card, the category list has Cashback & Rewards at the top. The receipt then says Cashback, not Refund.
