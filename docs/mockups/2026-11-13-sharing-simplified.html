Household sharing, simplified

Today sharing has 6 ideas (workspace share, net worth group, shared item, Joint, shared goal, shared event with its own code), a separate tax question, and switches on 10 different pages. The idea here: one setup, one list, three words. Names (Rina, Andi) and figures invented.

1 · Three words, for everything

WordMeans for AndiUsed for
PrivateDoesn't see it at allAccounts, cards, assets, debts, goals, events
BalanceSees how much, in the household's Net worth. Can't use it, doesn't see its rowsAccounts, cards, assets, debts
JointSees it, its rows, and can pay from it or add to it (Wallet card on his phone)Accounts, cards, goals, events

Goals and events have only Private / Joint: a joint goal is saved for together; a joint event is planned together. Budgets, bills and transactions in Family are always shared — said once, at setup.
