Household sharing, simplified

Today sharing has 6 ideas (workspace share, net worth group, shared item, Joint, shared goal, shared event with its own code), a separate tax question, and switches on ~10 pages. Here: one setup, one list, one switch (Shared), and Joint as a separate mark for accounts owned together. Names (Rina, Andi) and figures invented.
