Split a purchase in another currency

A hotel bill of USD 120,00 on an IDR card, charged Rp 1.944.000, split into the room and breakfast. Today a split refuses USD on the flag. Three ways to allow it. New parts outlined in orange. Invented hotel and figures.

AParts in rupiah

The flag stays on USD and the amount row keeps both figures. In Split, the parts are typed in rupiah and add up to the rupiah figure charged.

The receipt keeps USD 120,00 as the original amount of the whole purchase. The parts have no USD figure.

+ Simplest. Matches the card statement, which is in rupiah.

− Each part's dollar figure has to be worked out by hand.

BParts in USD

Same flag and amount row. In Split, the parts are typed in USD and add up to USD 120,00. Each part shows its rupiah figure under it, worked out at the same rate as the whole charge (here Rp 16.200 per dollar).

The last part takes any leftover rupiah from rounding, so the parts always add up to the exact figure charged.

+ Type straight from a receipt in dollars.

− More figures on screen. A part's rupiah can be one rupiah off a calculator.

CA and B together

Each part has both figures. Type either one: the other is worked out at the whole charge's rate and shown in grey with ≈. Typing over the grey one makes it the typed one instead.

The Total shows both figures, and the last part (the Rest) takes what is left in both.
