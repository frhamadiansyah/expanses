What the app does on Save, behind the scenes

Nothing to choose: the app checks the card's own rows.

RefundedIf the 15 Oct charge is in the app, the refund cancels it.
If it isn't (charged before the app), the refund is still added; see 3.
NeverOnly marks 2026 waived; next charge expected 15 Oct 2027.
ChargedDefault. Clears whatever was saved (asks first).
