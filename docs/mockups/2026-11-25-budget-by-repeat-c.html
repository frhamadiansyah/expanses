C · Budget typed by how it repeats, watched by the month

Each budget is typed the way it really comes: Rp 100.000 a week, Rp 50.000 a day, Rp 1.000.000 every 3 weeks, Rp 2.000.000 a month. The Budget page stays monthly: a month’s budget is the amount × how many times it comes up in that month. Over or not is judged for the month. New parts outlined in orange. Invented figures.
