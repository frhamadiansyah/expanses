Turn into instalments on a bond or stock bought with a card

Invented example: a retail government bond (ORI030), Rp 6.000.000 bought with BCA Visa on 10 Nov (statement day 18th), turned into 6 months at 0% = Rp 1.000.000 a month. Buying a bond is not spending, so its instalments never count as spending: each one is a card repayment. Red numbers = taps. Green NEW = what is added.
