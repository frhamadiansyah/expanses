iPhone Cashflow · yearly bills with no spike (ideas 1 + 2)

Invented: Life insurance Rp 12.000.000 a year, paid 5 March, set aside Rp 1.000.000 a month; Car insurance Rp 4.800.000, paid in December, Rp 400.000 a month. One rule: a bill set to Yearly is drawn on its own line under the ring, never inside it. It still counts in the month's total, in the year, and in every report, on its real date.

1 · A normal month

The ring is everyday spending, as today. A new row shows the month's set-aside for yearly bills: kept for the bill, not spending, so it doesn't change the ring or the total.
