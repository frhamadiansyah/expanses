Card instalments: real transactions and/or spreading the spending

Invented example: a laptop bought on the BCA Visa card on 10 Nov for Rp 12.000.000, converted to 12 monthly instalments of Rp 1.000.000 (0%). Statement day 18th. Three screens per option: the card's December statement, Cashflow November, Cashflow December. Green "NEW" = what changes.

The statement shows each month's instalment as a line worked out from the plan — grey, not a transaction (can't be tapped, matched by statement check, or seen in the card's list). Cashflow counts the laptop once, in November.

A · Real instalment transactions Recommended

On each statement day the app adds a real transaction on the card: "Laptop · instalment 1 of 12". It can be tapped, statement check can match it, and paying the card covers it. It is the repayment, not new spending — the laptop still counts once, in November. In Cashflow it is listed with the card's repayments (shown with the Transfers filter), not in spending.

B · Spread the spending (per plan)

A choice on the instalment plan: "Count spending: At purchase | Each month". With Each month, Cashflow counts Rp 1.000.000 in each of the 12 months instead of Rp 12.000.000 in November. The statement stays as today (worked-out lines).

How instalments connect to other pages (with A or C)

Today the laptop is hidden inside the card's balance on Debt-free, and the card's bill shows one amount with no hint of why it is higher. Proposed: each plan is its own row under its card, and the card's bill names what is inside it. Nothing is counted twice — the card's own row shows only the rest of the card balance.
