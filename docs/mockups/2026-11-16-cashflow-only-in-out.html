Cashflow lists only money in and money out

One rule, the same in Personal and Family, on the phone and the computer: Cashflow lists what counts as spending or income. Everything else (transfers, card payments, balance corrections, lending, starting balances, saving instalments) lives on the account, card or person page it belongs to — never deleted, just not in Cashflow.
