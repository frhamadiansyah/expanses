Mac redesign · step 5: Bills

Invented household, sidebar A, the same style as Overview/Cashflow/Budget. Everything the iPhone's Bills page has stays: due soon, monthly bills, instalments, paid, paused, set aside for yearly bills, and paying several at once. A List / Calendar switch in the title bar.

List

Three boxes: how much of this month is left to pay, the next 7 days (late first), and money set aside for yearly bills. Below, every bill in one table grouped like the phone (Due soon · Later this month · Paid · Paused) with a status column. Tick bills and "Pay 2 bills" pays them together; clicking a bill opens it in the side peek (history, Pay, Edit, Pause).
