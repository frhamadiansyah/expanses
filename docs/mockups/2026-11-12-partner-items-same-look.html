Net worth: the partner’s accounts look like one’s own

On the household’s Net worth, the other person’s accounts wear a coloured ring and open a different, plainer page. Proposed: the same rows and the same account page as one’s own, with the owner shown as a small initial on the icon’s corner — on one’s own accounts too. What the other person keeps private stays private: only household purchases and one “other use” total show. Names, accounts and amounts here are made up (Budi and Rina).
