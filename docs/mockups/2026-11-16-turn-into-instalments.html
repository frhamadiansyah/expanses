Turn a purchase into instalments: one step, nothing typed twice

Invented example: Laptop, Rp 12.000.000 on BCA Visa on 10 Nov (statement day 18th), turned into 6 months at 0% = Rp 2.000.000 a month. The purchase is already there (read from the bank's SMS or added by hand). Red numbers = taps. Green NEW = what is added.

A · From the purchase itself Recommended

Open the purchase (from Cashflow, the card's list or its statement). A credit card purchase gets one new row, "Instalments". Tap it, choose the months, Save. Name, amount, card, purchase and first bill month are filled in from the purchase.
