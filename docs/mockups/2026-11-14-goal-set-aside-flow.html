Set aside, from the goal page: pick, then amount

What is awkward today, and what this flow fixes:

  • After picking an account, it is added as a row at the bottom of the form: scroll down to find it. After: picking opens the amount screen straight away, nothing to scroll.
  • How much is free hides behind ⓘ. After: free money is on the right of every account in the list, and again right above the amount.
  • Too much can be saved, leaving the account short. After: Save is blocked until the amount fits what is free.
  • The amount box needs a tap first. After: the amount screen opens with the number pad already up.

Invented figures. Goal "Hajj 2030", target Rp 120.000.000. Mandiri Tabungan: Rp 6.500.000 free.
