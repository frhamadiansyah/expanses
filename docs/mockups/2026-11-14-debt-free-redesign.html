Debt-free plan: before and after

Before = screenshots of the app today (test figures). After = one page holding what now takes four: what is left and when it ends, the next payment, the order, extra a month, and the list. Schedule and Report stay as two rows at the bottom. Invented figures.

Before (today)

Main page

  • An empty 0% ring and "repaid Rp 0 of Rp 37.570.000" repeat the big figure.
  • "0 late · 0 paid off" counts say nothing most months.
  • A full-width black button for one payment.
  • Then four rows to four more pages: the plan itself is one tap away.

Pay first

  • A paragraph explaining all three orders, then a blue box.
  • Debt-free month repeated from the main page.
  • A black Pay button on every row; grey lines cut off ("left …").
  • Dewi's row has no amount, so rows don't line up.

Schedule · Report

  • Schedule: three tiles for one payment; "Statem…" cut off; a "B" letter instead of the card.
  • Report: "Nothing due" grey pills.

After Proposed

One page, top to bottom: when it ends · what to pay next · the order and extra · the debts in that order · Schedule and Report. Pay only where a payment is due; every row one line wide in the same columns.
