Paste a list: dates

Two fixes. (1) The reader learns Indonesian month words and dates written without a space: 21okt, 21 okt, 21-okt, 3 agt, 5 mei, 1 des, oktober 21 are all read. (2) It is always clear which date each line gets: on the paste screen, and on Review, where rows sit under their day. Items and amounts here are made up.
