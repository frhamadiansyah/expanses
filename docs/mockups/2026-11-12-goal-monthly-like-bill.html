Goal Monthly that works like a bill: Set aside or Skip each month

Nothing happens by itself. Each month the goal’s Monthly amount comes due, like a bill, and waits for a tap: Set aside (money stays in the account, moves from Free to spend to Set aside) or Transfer (money moves to a savings account), or Skip this month. Names and amounts are made up.
