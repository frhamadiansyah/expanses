Paid off, in part

“Paid off” stays one tap for the whole amount, as today. Only when the amount paid before the card was added is less than the purchase is anything typed. Example: a Rp3.000.000 purchase on 15 Oct, Rp1.800.000 of it paid before the card was added. New parts outlined in orange. Invented names and figures.
