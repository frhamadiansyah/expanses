How a transfer shows it is an ATM withdrawal

No category and no new field. The app names a transfer by the two accounts it already has: money going into a Cash account from a bank or e-wallet reads Cash withdrawal. The note stays free for the ATM's name. A category is not used because a category would count the cash as spending, and then every purchase paid with that cash would be counted a second time. All figures invented.
